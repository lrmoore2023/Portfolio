ABOUT

I take AI capabilities from research to shipped systems.

At Stability AI I work on the Applied Research team, investigating what new models can do across diffusion and pixel-space image models, LLMs, and VLMs. I design the post-training experiments that test them (SFT, full fine-tuning, GRPO, RLHF with a human in the loop), build the evaluations that decide go or no-go, and turn what works into trained models and production pipelines for clients.

Before Stability I built AI tooling for creative organizations. At Vidsy I shipped AI-driven internal tools in rapid prototyping sprints, with the ones the creative team adopted handed to Engineering for full build-out. At Saatchi & Saatchi I designed AI workflows for client projects and contributed to campaign work that won a Bronze Lion at Cannes 2025.

My background is in design and creative technology, and it shapes how I build: I prototype quickly and design tools to fit the way teams already work. That blend of research, engineering, and design carries through everything I ship, from trained models to internal tools and AI workflows.
